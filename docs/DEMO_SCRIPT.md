# Demo script (about 3 minutes)

## Before you present

1. Open https://claimsathi-web.onrender.com and click **Try sample case** to wake the API. It can take up to a minute.
2. Download the three sample PDFs from the home page.
3. Upload them once as a test. If this says busy, plan to use the sample case only.
4. Keep the backup video and screenshots open in another tab.

## 1. The problem (30 seconds)

"When an insurer cuts a health claim, the family gets a letter full of clauses. They can't tell what's fair, so many just accept it. ClaimSathi tells them what looks wrong and helps them appeal."

## 2. Upload (30 seconds)

Click **Analyse claim** with the three sample PDFs.

"These are made-up documents. AI is reading them now. Everything it finds gets checked by tested code."

It usually takes under a minute. If it's slow, say: "We're on a free AI plan, so while that runs, here's a finished case," and click **Try sample case**.

## 3. The result (45 seconds)

Point at **₹71,400 likely recoverable out of ₹96,000**.

"Here's what this family could challenge." Open the first row's evidence.

"This shows the insurer's own words and the policy clause. On a PDF, this tick means we found the exact quote in the document, with no AI involved. It stops the AI inventing quotes."

## 4. The rules decide (45 seconds)

In **Confirm the facts**, set **Continuous cover date** to 1 Jan 2024, then click **Apply corrections**.

"AI can misread a date, so the user has the final say. Now the waiting period isn't over, so ₹20,600 stops being marked wrong, and recoverable falls to ₹50,800. That was instant, with no AI call. The same facts always give the same answer."

Switch to **हिंदी**. "It's all available in Hindi too."

## 5. Taking action (30 seconds)

Scroll to the letters and click **Draft complaint to insurer**.

"It writes the complaint from those findings, and the user can edit it and save it as a PDF. Deadlines come from the official Ombudsman Rules, and they can be added to a calendar."

## 6. Close (15 seconds)

"The AI reads, the rules judge. It's a working product today, and next we want to confirm every legal reference and test it on real, consented documents."

## If something fails

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Upload says busy | Click **Try sample case**: "The free AI is busy, so here's a finished case." |
| Result shows **Sample case** after upload | Say it: "The AI was busy, so this is our saved reading, and the app labels it." |
| API is slow | "The free server is waking up." Then wait. |
| Internet fails | Play the backup video. |

## Backup video (record once, about 2 minutes)

Record sections 2 to 5 using Windows' Xbox Game Bar: press `Win + G`, then click record. Save a copy on your phone too.
